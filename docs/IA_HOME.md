# Homepage information architecture

**Goal:** One job, one primary CTA. Repeat a fact at most twice on a page. A third time is a link.

**Positioning:** Nationwide AI print-to-mail + online Certified Mail. Three seats: lockup **AI print to mail** · slogan **You upload. You approve. We produce.** · comprehension **Print to mail, made easy** (reserved, never H1). Wichita only on About / footer / schema address.

## Home sections (exactly 6)

| # | Section | Content |
|---|---------|---------|
| 1 | Hero | H1 = trust slogan (`You upload. You approve. We produce.`). Lead is the value, not a second title: files become real mail. We deliver them to USPS. USPS delivers nationwide. No wordmark, no “platform,” no slogan echo. |
| 2 | What you get | Print (incl. document split) · Mail Piece(s) (addressed, stuffed envelope(s) or postcard(s)) · USPS (postage applied, delivered to USPS on mail date). Tracing, logo envelope, NCOA, tracking live on `/features`. Approve stays in the lead and how-it-works. No MCP on home. |
| 3 | How it works | Process diagram (Upload → Approve → We produce → USPS) + **See the full process** → `/how-it-works`. Not a second card grid. |
| 4 | Trust | Real mail. USPS delivers nationwide. `/about`. No Wichita on home. No 50-years heritage on home. |
| 5 | FAQ | **6** questions max (what / available / upload / Certified / statements / billing); all closed |
| 6 | Get started | App **Get started** + Contact us form + one primary phone + email. 316 lives on `/contact` / About / legal. |

## CTA hierarchy (site-wide)

1. **Primary:** Get started → `https://app.usmail.ai/`
2. **Secondary:** How it works / docs links (ghost)
3. **Human path:** Contact form on home and `/contact`. Phone in the footer. Home Get started may show `888-667-5322` once. Not on every CTA. `316` only About / contact / legal. Footer email stays.

Nav: How it works · Features · Print & mail · Certified Mail · Industries · Get started.

Footer always includes Features, Compare, and Contact.

## Do not re-add

- Dual hero paragraphs / AEO dump beside lead
- Wichita or “mailing services Wichita” in titles or H1
- EDDM
- Hour counts or USPS transit promises
- “Learn more” 4-card hub under hero
- Formats grid on home
- 8 industry chips on home
- 11-item FAQ
- Equal dual-phone CTA clusters
