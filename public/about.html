About USMail.ai

Nationwide AI print-to-mail. Run by experienced print-to-mail experts. Innovation through AI.

Who we are

USMail.ai is a nationwide AI print-to-mail product. It is run by a team of experienced print-to-mail experts with more than 50 years of combined expertise, now providing print-to-mail innovation through AI.

You upload. You approve. We print, insert, apply postage, and hand off to USPS. We are a mail house — not a developer API and not an agent wrapper over Click2Mail or Lob.

Service is nationwide. Wichita, Kansas is the command center, not a walk-in shop.

We do not publish customer names on this page. Talk to mail ops if you need references.

What is live

Self-serve

  • Portal: upload, approve, produce, USPS
  • Account + prepaid meter; AI setup you approve
  • Certified Mail / CoM on the same path as First-Class

People + agents

  • Mail ops by phone and email
  • MCP for agents on the same production path (account required)
  • Cutoffs and IMB tracing — How it works

Not the U.S. Postal Service

USMail.ai is not the U.S. Postal Service and is not affiliated with USPS. We prepare physical mail and hand off to USPS for delivery.

Not US Global Mail

US Global Mail is a virtual mailbox. USMail.ai does not receive your inbound post. We print outbound mail you upload and enter it with USPS.
