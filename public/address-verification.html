What we claim — and do not

  • CASS and NCOA Included

    CASS standardizes the address. NCOA (National Change of Address) runs with production before USPS handoff.

  • Fewer returns

    Updated addresses reduce wasted postage and returned pieces. Not zero returns.

  • Not a delivery SLA

    We do not promise USPS days-in-transit. Verification is not tracking.

FAQ

Does USMail.ai verify addresses?

Yes. NCOA is included. Address verification runs on the production path, including change-of-address processing, before print. The goal is fewer returned pieces. It is not a guarantee that every piece will deliver.

Does address verification speed up USPS delivery?

No. Verification reduces undeliverable mail. It does not change USPS transit time. USMail.ai does not publish delivery-day promises.
