Definition

What is AI print to mail?

AI print to mail is automated physical mailing: you upload documents and a list; software proposes addresses and print options; you approve; a production plant prints, inserts, applies postage, and hands the pieces to USPS. USMail.ai is a nationwide platform for that. Not the U.S. Postal Service.

The five steps

  1. 1. Upload

    PDF, Word, images, packages, CSV lists.

  2. 2. AI setup

    Addresses and print options — proposed, not auto-sent.

  3. 3. You approve

    Nothing produces until a human confirms.

  4. 4. Produce

    Print, insert, postage on the Postalocity stack.

  5. 5. USPS handoff

    First-Class or Certified on the scheduled mail date.
