When proof matters

Formal notices, compliance mail, and anything that cannot afford a weak paper trail.

  • Certified Mail Delivery-oriented USPS proof for pieces that need a stronger trail than ordinary First-Class.
  • Certificate of Mailing Documentation that the piece was presented to USPS for mailing — use when your process requires it.
