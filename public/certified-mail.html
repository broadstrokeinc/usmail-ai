Certified vs Certificate of Mailing vs First-Class

Pick the service your process needs — plain language only; counsel decides legal sufficiency.

Mail option What it is for On USMail.ai
First-Class Everyday letters and notices when ordinary delivery is enough. Default letter production path.
Certified Mail Delivery-oriented USPS proof for pieces that need a stronger trail. Selectable option on the same upload → produce flow.
Certificate of Mailing Documentation that a piece was presented to USPS for mailing. Selectable when your process calls for presentation proof.

No green-card claims here — options match what production actually supports.
