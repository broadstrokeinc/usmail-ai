What’s in the product

  • Upload

    PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), PNG/JPG, packages, CSV lists.

  • AI setup

    Addresses and print options proposed for the files you send. You still approve.

  • Print options

    Black and white or color. Simplex or duplex. Insert, address, postage on our side.

  • Mail options

    First-Class. Certified Mail. Certificate of Mailing. Certified is never same-day.

  • Cutoffs

    Same-day when the job qualifies (1:00 PM CT). Default next business day (10:00 PM CT). Schedule up to 60 days. Mon–Fri. Rules →

  • Tracing vs proof

    IMB tracing on qualifying domestic First-Class — not legal proof. Certified and Certificate of Mailing are proof. Paid USPS tracking is separate. Tracing →

  • Address verification

    On the production path to reduce returned mail. Not a USPS delivery-day promise. Address verification →

    Statements and invoices as uploaded files and lists. You approve each job. Statements →
