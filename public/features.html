Features FAQ

What makes USMail.ai different?

Four things on this path. Tracing is included on qualifying First-Class letters. Tracking is an option on Priority and Certified Mail. Custom envelopes with your logo are included; they must be approved before use and can take 1–2 business days. NCOA is included. Tracing is not tracking, and neither one is Certified proof.

Is tracing included on First-Class?

Yes. IMB tracing on qualifying domestic First-Class letters. Not legal proof. How tracing works →

Can I add tracking on Priority or Certified Mail?

Yes. USPS tracking is an option on Priority and Certified Mail. That is not First-Class tracing.

Can I use a custom envelope with my logo?

Yes. Custom envelopes with your logo are included. They must be approved before use and can take 1–2 business days. Same-day jobs still have to qualify on a plain #10 or Utah envelope.

What is included per mail piece?

CASS, NCOA, envelope, paper, tracing, and postage. Tracing is IMB on qualifying First-Class pieces and is not legal proof. The meter is debited when you approve.

Can I split one file into many pieces?

Yes. Split or cut a document into mail pieces by page, by text, or by logo. Scan addresses from the file, import a CSV, or add addresses by hand. You choose how duplicate names and addresses are handled.

Is Certified available on postcards?

No. Certified is not available for postcards. Choose First-Class or Priority for a postcard. Tracking is an option on Priority and Certified Mail.

How long is a job stored? What is Posty AI?

Jobs are stored by default for 30 days at no extra storage charge. Posty AI chats with you at every step and can propose setup. A human still approves before production.
