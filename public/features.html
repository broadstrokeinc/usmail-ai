What we offer

Four USPs. Tracing is not tracking. Tracking is not Certified proof.

  • Tracing on First-Class

    IMB mail-piece tracing on qualifying domestic First-Class letters. Included. Not legal proof. How tracing works →

  • Tracking on Priority and Certified

    USPS tracking as an option on Priority and Certified letters. Not on ordinary First-Class tracing. Certified →

  • Custom envelope with your logo

    Included. Add a logo and brand the envelope. Same-day still has to qualify (plain #10 or Utah).

  • NCOA included

    National Change of Address runs on the production path. Fewer returned pieces. Not a USPS delivery-day promise. Address verification →

What this is not

  • Not USPS

    We prepare and enter the mail. USPS delivers.

  • Not EDDM

    Not Every Door Direct Mail.

  • Not a billing system

    We do not generate statements inside your core or ERP.

  • Not a public MCP

    No anonymous mailing. No invented add command.
