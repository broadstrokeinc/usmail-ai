Get started Certified Mail

The five steps

Five steps. You upload. We produce and hand off to USPS.

  1. Upload

    PDF, Word, images, multi-file packages, recipient lists — formats you already use.

  2. AI setup

    Addresses, print options — then you approve before production.

  3. Choose mail options

    First-Class; optional Certified or Certificate of Mailing for formal proof. Tracing is separate (see below).

  4. We produce

    Print, insert, address, postage — no supplies on your side.

  5. USPS handoff

    On your scheduled mail date (Mon–Fri). Cutoffs below decide same-day vs next business day.

Mail-piece tracing & tracking

Three different capabilities — don’t mix them up.

Mail-piece tracing (IMB)

Included

Free status on qualifying domestic First-Class #10 pieces — barcode scans through out for delivery, viewable per piece in the job.

Not legal proof · not international · good address required

Paid USPS tracking

Extra cost

Formal USPS tracking with services such as Certified or Priority — beyond free IMB tracing.
