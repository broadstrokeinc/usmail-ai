The five steps

Five steps. You upload. We produce and hand off to USPS.

  1. Upload

    PDF, Word, images, multi-file packages, recipient lists — formats you already use.

  2. AI setup

    Addresses, print options — then you approve before production.

  3. Choose mail options

    First-Class; optional Certified or Certificate of Mailing for formal proof. Tracing is separate (see below).

  4. We produce

    Print, insert, address, postage — no supplies on your side.

  5. USPS handoff

    On your scheduled mail date (Mon–Fri). Cutoffs below decide same-day vs next business day.

How it works FAQ

How does USMail.ai work?

Five steps: upload your documents; AI helps set up addresses and print options — you approve; choose mail options; we print, insert, and apply postage; we hand the pieces to USPS on your mail date.

What are the production cutoffs?

Same-day when the job qualifies: approve by 1:00 PM Central, Mon–Fri. Default path: approve by 10:00 PM Central for the next business-day mail date. Certified is never same-day. Details in the cutoff cards above.

Do mail pieces include tracing or tracking?

Tracing is included on qualifying First-Class letters. Tracking is an option on Priority and Certified letters. Certified proof is not tracing.
