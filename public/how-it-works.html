The six steps

No mailroom stack on your side. No meters. No guesswork at the counter.

  1. Upload PDF, Word, images, multi-file packages, recipient lists — formats you already use.
  2. AI setup Addresses, zones, B&W or color, simplex or duplex — then you approve.
  3. Pick products First-Class, plus Certified or Certificate of Mailing when needed.
  4. We produce Print, insert, address, postage — no supplies on your side.
  5. USPS handoff In by 10:00 PM Central (CT) for next business-day mail path.
  6. Track status USPS tracking on Priority & Certified. Job status end to end.
