FAQ

Questions people ask

What is USMail.ai?
  • USMail.ai is a nationwide AI print-to-mail platform.
  • You upload documents and postcards.
  • AI proposes setup.
  • You approve.
  • We print, insert, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS.
  • Online Certified Mail and statement or invoice jobs use the same path.
  • USMail.ai is not the United States Postal Service.
What is AI print to mail?

AI print to mail means you upload documents and postcards. Software proposes setup. You approve. We print, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS. USPS delivers nationwide. AI print to mail →

Is USMail.ai the US Postal Service?

No. USMail.ai is a private print-to-mail product. We prepare the mail and give it to USPS. USPS delivers it. This is not a USPS website. About →

Can I send Certified Mail online?

Yes. Add Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing before you approve. We print the document, add the service, and deliver the piece to USPS on the same job path as First-Class. Certified is never same-day. Tracking is optional on Certified Mail; it is not First-Class tracing, and tracing is not proof. Online Certified Mail →

Can I mail statements and invoices?

Yes. Upload the statement or invoice files your billing system already makes, plus a list if you need one. You approve each job. We do not generate bills inside your system. Statements & invoices →

Is USMail.ai available now?

Yes. Create an account in the app, fund a prepaid meter, and send a job. Get started.
