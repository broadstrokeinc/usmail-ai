How it works

From upload to USPS — production stays with us so your team stays focused.

  1. Upload any supported format

    PDF, Word, images, and more — single files or multi-document packages. Create content online or drop what you already have.

  2. AI assists setup

    AI helps extract recipients, map zones, and guide print options (B&W or color, simplex or duplex) before you submit.

  3. Choose mailing products

    First-Class letter mail with optional Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing when you need formal proof.

  4. We print, insert & postage

    Production print, automatic insert into envelopes, professional addressing, and postage applied — no meters or inventory on your floor.

  5. USPS handoff

    Completed mail-pieces go to the U.S. Postal Service. Target path: in by 10:00 PM CST, prepared for next business-day mail.

  6. Track status

    Monitor mailing status with tracking visibility through the delivery process.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for search, assistants, and evaluators.

What is USMail.AI?

An AI-enabled online mailing platform: upload documents from your desk; we print, insert, postage, and deliver via USPS — with Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing options.

What file formats can I upload?

Common formats include PDF, Microsoft Word (DOC/DOCX), images (PNG, JPG), multi-file packages, and recipient lists (CSV/spreadsheet) for list mail. Ask for the current accepted set for your product.

Does USMail.AI support Certified Mail?

Yes — Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing are supported for correspondence that needs formal proof of mailing.

How does AI help?

AI assists with document understanding, recipient address extraction, setup guidance, and quality checks so you spend less time preparing mail manually.

What is the USMail.AI MCP?

Model Context Protocol (MCP) lets AI agents and tools call mailing capabilities programmatically — create and submit physical mail from automation, not only the UI.

What is the cutoff for next business-day mail?

Target path: submit by 10:00 PM Central for next business-day preparation toward USPS (handoff window around 6:30 PM CST the next business day). Confirm for your volume.
