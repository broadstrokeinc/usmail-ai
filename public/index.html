FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers. No fluff. Built for buyers, search, and AI assistants.

What is USMail.AI?

An AI-native mailing platform (coming soon). You upload letters and documents; we print, insert into real envelopes, apply postage, and hand off to USPS — with Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing when proof matters.

Is USMail.AI available now?

Coming soon. Contact us for a demonstration and early access — 888-667-5322 or 316-247-5300, or Info@USMAIL.ai.

Do I create documents here — or upload them?

You upload. Bring PDFs, Word files, images, packages, and recipient lists you already have. We handle production — not document authoring.

What formats can I upload?

The formats you already use: PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), images (PNG/JPG), multi-file packages, and recipient CSV / spreadsheets for list mail. Mixed packages welcome. Ask for the full accepted list for your job type — we’ll be explicit.

Do you support Certified Mail?

Yes — fully. Choose Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing when formal proof is non-negotiable: legal notices, compliance mail, anything that cannot afford a weak paper trail.

How does AI actually help?

AI does the grunt work of setup: reading uploaded PDFs, extracting recipient addresses, guiding print options, and flagging quality issues before production. You approve. We mail. Faster path from file to USPS — not chatbot theater.

What is the USMail.AI MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets AI agents and automation submit mailing jobs programmatically — the same production path as the portal, without a manual mailroom.

Who is USMail.AI for?

Primary: credit repair, banks, cities & counties. Also debt collection, healthcare, storage facilities, utilities, legal, business ops, and AI/MCP teams — few pieces or high volume. Industries →

What is the cutoff for next business-day mail?

Submit by 10:00 PM Central (CT) for the next business-day production path toward USPS. Confirm for your volume and product.

How does billing work?

Account + prepaid meter at product open. Portal and MCP debit the meter. Standalone API keys for agents are planned later.
