How it works

You upload documents. We handle production and USPS delivery.

  1. Upload your documents

    PDF, Word, images, and more — single files or multi-document packages. Bring the files you already have; you don’t author the letter in USMail.AI.

  2. AI assists setup

    AI helps extract recipients, map address zones, and guide print options (B&W or color, simplex or duplex) before you submit.

  3. Choose mailing products

    First-Class letter mail with optional Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing when you need formal proof.

  4. We print, insert & postage

    Production print, automatic insert into envelopes, professional addressing, and postage applied — no meters or supplies required on your side.

  5. USPS handoff

    Completed mail-pieces go to the U.S. Postal Service. Target path: in by 10:00 PM CST, prepared for next business-day mail.

  6. Track status

    Monitor mailing status with tracking visibility through the delivery process.

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers. No fluff. Built for buyers, search, and AI assistants.

What is USMail.AI — in one sentence?

Automated print-to-mail. You upload letters and documents; we print, insert into real envelopes, apply postage, and hand off to USPS — with Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing when proof matters. No mailroom. No meters. No “we’ll get to it.”

Do I create documents here — or upload them?

You upload. Always. Bring the PDFs, Word files, images, packages, and recipient lists you already have. USMail.AI is production print-to-mail — not a word processor. Upload. Print. Mail.

What formats can I upload?

The formats you already use: PDF, Word (DOC/DOCX), images (PNG/JPG), multi-file packages, and recipient CSV / spreadsheets for list mail. Mixed packages welcome. Ask for the full accepted list for your job type — we’ll be explicit.

Do you support Certified Mail?

Yes — fully. Choose Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing when formal proof is non-negotiable: legal notices, compliance mail, anything that cannot afford a weak paper trail.

How does AI actually help?

AI does the grunt work of setup: reading uploaded PDFs, extracting recipient addresses, guiding print options, and flagging quality issues before production. You approve. We mail. Faster path from file to USPS — not chatbot theater.

What is the USMail.AI MCP?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is how AI agents and automation call print-to-mail as a tool. Same production path as the portal — upload, setup, submit — without a human mailroom in the middle. Built for teams already running LLMs.

Who is this for?

Anyone who must send physical mail reliably: individuals and professionals, small businesses, corporations, nonprofits, firms, cities, counties, utilities, and agencies. Invoices, notices, notifications, compliance mail — a few pieces or high volume.

What is the cutoff for next business-day mail?

Submit by 10:00 PM Central for the next business-day production path toward USPS (target handoff around 6:30 PM CST the next business day). Miss the post office line. Hit our cutoff instead. Confirm for your volume and product.
