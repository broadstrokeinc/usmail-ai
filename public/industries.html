One production path. Not a vertical SaaS.

Credit repair, a bank, a county, a clinic, and a storage facility send different documents. They use the same job: upload, proposed setup, human approve, produce, deliver to USPS. You keep the system of record. We keep production.

We are not a CRM. Not an EHR. Not a property management system. Not a CIS. Not core banking. If your stack already writes the PDF, the Word file, or the list, you are in the right place. If you need software to create the dispute, the chart, the ledger, or the bill, that is not this product.

Volume does not change the path. A one-off notice and a recurring statement file both wait for a human on the app. The prepaid meter debits when you approve. Production mail is not free. Features that come with the piece — envelope, paper, tracing on qualifying First-Class, logo envelope when approved — live on Features, not here.

  • You upload

    Files your system already makes. Recurring jobs and one-off notices use the same path.

  • Software proposes

    Setup is proposed, not auto-sent. A human still confirms on the app.

  • We produce

    Print, insert, postage. Then we deliver the pieces to USPS. USPS delivers the mail.

  • You keep the system

    Core, EHR, PMS, CIS, CRM — those stay yours. We do not replace them.

Address verification is included

CASS and NCOA run on every piece. Addresses are standardized and change-of-address runs before print. You review flagged pieces, then you approve. Fewer returns. Not a USPS delivery-time promise. On Certified Mail a flagged address cannot still-mail.

That check is not tracing and not Certified proof. Do not treat this hub as the CASS page.

Cutoffs sit on the process page

Same-day is a narrow First-Class path. Certified is never same-day. Times are Central. Mail dates are weekdays. The default path is next weekday when you approve by the evening cutoff. You can also schedule a later weekday.

Vertical pages do not invent their own clocks. If you need the eligibility list, use How it works.

Industries FAQ

Who does USMail.ai serve?

Senders who already produce documents and postcards: statements, invoices, notices, and Certified Mail. Primary verticals are credit repair, banks, and city and county government. Also collections, healthcare, storage, utilities, and legal. Same production path for all. We are not a vertical SaaS.

Does USMail.ai mail credit repair letters?

Yes. Credit repair is a primary segment. Upload dispute letters, bureau notices, client correspondence, and lists your shop already writes. Optional Certified Mail. We do not run a dispute CRM. Credit repair mailing →

Does USMail.ai mail for banks and government?

Yes. Banks and city and county government are primary segments. Upload notices, disclosures, and statement files your systems already generate. Optional Certified Mail. We do not replace core banking or a municipal system. Banks → · Government →

Does USMail.ai mail storage facility notices?

Yes. Upload lien, auction, and occupant notices your process already uses. Optional Certified Mail. We do not run a facility PMS. Storage mailing →
