FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail bank notices and statements?

Yes. Upload the notices, disclosures, and statement or invoice files your core already generates, plus a list if you need one. You approve. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS. We do not generate statements inside a core banking system.

No. You own the file. We own production. Upload files and lists. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS.

Can I add Certified Mail?

Yes. Add Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing before you approve and pay. Certified is never same-day.
