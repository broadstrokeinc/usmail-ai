What you send. What we do not.

You send the letter your agency already uses — demand, validation, settlement, or similar. You send the list. You approve the proof. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS.

We do not skip-trace. We do not score accounts. We do not write the notice. You and your collection system own the words. We own production.

Proof is a mail option, not a CRM feature

Add Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing before you approve and pay. That is a USPS proof product on the same job — not a second vendor, not a plugin, not an agent wrapper over someone else’s mail house.

FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail collection notices?

Yes. Upload the notices your agency already generates, plus a list if you need one. You approve. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS. Add Certified Mail before you approve if you need proof. We do not write collection letters or run a CRM.

Is this a collections CRM?

No. We do not skip-trace, score accounts, or write collection letters. You and your collection system own the words. You upload the notice and the list. A human still approves before we print.

Can I add Certified Mail?

Yes. Add Certified Mail before you approve and pay.

Are NCOA and logo envelopes included?

Yes. NCOA and custom logo envelopes are included. Tracing is not Certified. Features →
