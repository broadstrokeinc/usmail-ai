What credit repair still puts in the mail

  • Bureau disputes

    Multi-page packages your shop already writes. We do not write the dispute.

  • Client correspondence

    Status and ops letters as files you own, plus a list if you need one.

  • Certified when proof is required

    Add Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing before you approve. Same path as First-Class.

  • Not a dispute CRM

    We do not score files, pull bureaus, or manage clients. We mail what you upload.

Desk print vs production

Printer supplies, envelopes, and a trip to the counter do not scale when the list is long. Upload the package and the list. You approve. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS.

You own the words

FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail credit repair letters?

Yes. Upload dispute letters, bureau notices, and client correspondence your shop already writes, plus a list if you need one. You approve. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS. Add Certified Mail before you approve if you need proof. We do not run a dispute CRM.

Is this a credit-repair CRM or dispute engine?

No. You own the words. We own production. Upload files and lists. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS.

Can I add Certified Mail?

Yes. Add Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing before you approve and pay. Same path as First-Class. Certified is never same-day.

Are NCOA and logo envelopes included?

Yes. NCOA and custom logo envelopes are included. Tracing is not Certified. Features →
