FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail city and county notices?

Yes. Upload resident notices, bills, and formal documents your department already generates, plus a list if you need one. You approve. We print, apply postage, and deliver to USPS. We are not agenda software.

Is this agenda or case software?

No. Upload files and lists. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS. We do not run meetings, tickets, or case files.

Certified Mail?

Yes. Add Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing before you approve if you need proof. You approve the proof. Certified is never same-day.
