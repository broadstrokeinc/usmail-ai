Billing already made the file. A person still approves.

Patient statements leave billing as PDFs and recipient lists. That is the file we take. We do not open the EHR. We do not post charges. We do not clear a claim. Recurring jobs use the same production path as a one-off notice. You still confirm each run.

Software can propose addresses and print options. An agent can set a job up. A human on the app still approves before anything prints. Agents cannot skip approve. That is the control for a file that has a patient name on it. Volume is not an excuse to skip the click.

Keep cycle statements and one-off patient notices on separate jobs when the documents differ. A monthly statement file is not an appointment notice. A coverage letter is not an invoice. Billing already split those exports. Do not recombine them here.

What this is not

  • Not an EHR

    We do not generate charts or clinical notes. We mail the file billing already made.

  • Not a clearinghouse

    We do not send claims. We do not sit between you and a payer.

  • Not a billing system

    Balances, adjustments, and statement generation stay in billing.

  • Not a skip-approve agent

    Agents can propose a job. They cannot skip approve or debit the meter. A person still clicks yes.

Healthcare mail FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail healthcare statements?

Yes. Patient statements and notices as files your billing system already makes, plus a list if you need one. A person on the app approves. We print, insert, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS. We are not an EHR.

Is USMail.ai an EHR, a clearinghouse, or a billing system?

No. We do not generate charts, claims, or statements inside an EHR. We do not clear claims. We do not replace billing. Upload the PDFs and lists billing already exports. Recurring statement jobs →
