FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail legal notices?

Yes. Upload demand letters, notices, and other PDFs you already have. You approve the proof. We print, insert, apply postage, and hand the pieces to USPS. We do not serve court papers, run your calendar, or practice law.

Is this legal advice or a case system?

No. We do not practice law or run a case system. You own the words. You upload the file. You approve the proof before anything prints.

Certified vs tracing vs tracking?

You pick Certified Mail or Certificate of Mailing. You approve the proof. First-Class tracing is included on qualifying pieces and is not Certified proof. Tracking is an option on Priority and Certified Mail.

Are NCOA and logo envelopes included?

Yes. NCOA and custom logo envelopes are included. Certified is never same-day. Features →
