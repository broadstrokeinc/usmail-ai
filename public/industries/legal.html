What law firms still put in the mail

The file is already in the matter. The piece still has to leave on paper.

  • Demand letters

    The demand PDF counsel already signed. We print that file. We do not draft it. If an exhibit is missing, that is the file you sent.

  • Notices

    Correspondence the matter already holds. Upload it. Review the setup. Confirm. We do not choose the addressee from a docket.

  • Engagement letters

    Engagement PDFs you already have. Production is print and postage, not intake.

  • Status PDFs

    Status updates as documents the file already contains. Batch with a list when you need to.

Nothing mails until a person says yes

The document is already in the matter. Software can read an address and propose print options. A person on the app still has to say yes. That is the point for legal correspondence: no silent auto-mail of a notice nobody reviewed.

Process, filing, and deadlines stay with the firm. We print, insert, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS after that yes. The mail date is a production window. It is not a docket entry.
