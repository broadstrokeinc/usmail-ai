What this is not

  • Not a drafter

    We do not draft lien copy. Auction language stays in the document you already use.

  • Not a PMS

    Units, gates, and ledgers stay in the facility system. We mail the export.

  • Not unit control

    We do not lock a unit or post a sale. Mail is the job.

  • Not an auction calendar

    We do not docket sale dates. The facility’s process keeps the clock.

Storage mail FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail storage lien notices?

Yes. Lien, auction, and occupant notices your process already uses, plus a list if you need one. You approve. We print, insert, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS. Add Certified Mail before you approve if the process wants proof. We do not manage units or ledgers.

Do you draft the lien or auction letter?

No. We do not draft lien copy. We do not write the auction notice. The facility already has the document the process uses. We print that file.

Can Certified lien mail go out same-day?

No. Same-day is never Certified. If the process wants Certified Mail, add it before you approve. The next business-day path applies. Cutoffs → · Certified Mail →

Is this a facility property-management system?

No. We do not manage units, gates, or ledgers. Occupancy, locks, and balances stay in the PMS. Export the occupant document and list. A person approves. We produce. A facility system of record is a different product.
