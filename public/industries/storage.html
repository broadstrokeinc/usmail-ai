FAQ

Does USMail.ai mail storage lien notices?

Yes. Upload lien, auction, and occupant notices your process already uses. You approve. We print, apply postage, and hand off to USPS. Add Certified Mail before you approve if you need proof. We do not manage units or ledgers.

Is this a storage management system?

No. We do not manage units, gates, or ledgers. Export the occupant document and list from your PMS. You approve. We produce.

Certified Mail?

Yes. Add Certified Mail before you approve and pay.

Are NCOA and logo envelopes included?

Yes. NCOA is included on occupant lists. Facility logo envelopes are included. Custom envelopes must be approved before use and can take 1–2 business days. Same-day is only eligible First-Class — never Certified. Check cutoffs before you promise an auction date. We do not publish USPS transit days.
