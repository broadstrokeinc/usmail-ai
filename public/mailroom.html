What leaves your office

Production work leaves. The copier tray is not a plant.

An office mailroom is a queue. Someone prints a stack. Someone folds. Someone stuffs envelopes. Someone meters postage. Someone drives to the drop. That queue is production. It does not belong next to the copier.

A copier is for the one-off you need in the room. A tray of statements, invoices, or notices is a production job. Production wants a mail date, a class, and a human who approves the stack. It does not want a desk that runs out of envelopes at 4:40.

  • Print / fold / insert

    We print documents and postcards on production equipment. Folding and inserting happen on our side for envelope jobs. You do not stand at a folder.

  • Postage and drop

    We apply postage. We deliver the pieces to USPS. You skip the post-office line.

  • Coordination

    First-Class and Certified use the same job. You do not send proof mail to a second vendor.

  • Recurring volume

    Statements, invoices, and notices can leave this way. You still approve each job. Statements & invoices →

Supplies, the window, and the shop

A desk mailroom buys stock. A walk-in shop is still a counter.

A desk mailroom buys paper, envelopes, toner, and postage. It also buys a trip to the window. Production paper and envelope come with the job. Postage is applied on our side. The prepaid meter is debited when you approve. We do not publish a supply-cost number.

No tray in the car. No window line. After you approve, the drop is our job. USPS delivers nationwide.

A walk-in shop is local. You often print, then mail. Here you upload one job. We print, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS. That is not a counter. Compare catalog, shop, and USPS →

What stays with you

Files stay yours. Approval stays yours. Staffing stays yours.

Files

Yours

PDF, Word, images, and CSV lists stay yours. We do not rewrite the document you uploaded. We do not generate bills inside a core or ERP.

Approval

A human

AI proposes setup. Nothing prints until a human confirms the job in front of you. Last month’s run does not mail this month.

The office

Not staffed

We do not staff your office mailroom. Incoming mail, the lobby, and the package cart stay yours. We take document and postcard production from upload to USPS.

Mailroom FAQ

Can USMail.ai replace an in-house mailroom?

For document and postcard production, yes. You upload files. We print, insert, apply postage, and deliver to USPS. We do not staff your office mailroom.

How much time does it save?

We do not publish a fixed hours-saved number. Volume and process decide. You skip desk print, fold, insert, postage, and the post-office run.
