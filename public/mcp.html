What MCP means here

Model Context Protocol is how modern AI systems expose tools. With USMail.AI, “mail this file” becomes a controlled action against production infrastructure — not a ticket into someone else’s mailroom.

Built for LLM-native teams, agent platforms, and operators who already run workflows in code or copilots — and still need physical mail that lands.

  • Agent upload & submit

    Documents and job parameters from the agent; human approval paths where you require them.

  • One backend

    Portal users and agents share print, insert, postage, and USPS handoff.

  • Proof products

    Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing available on the same stack when configured.

  • AI setup, not guesswork

    Address extraction and print guidance before production — for people and agents.

Who it’s for

Sharp wedge for teams that already think in tools and workflows.

  • AI / platform teams

    Expose physical mail as a capability next to email, CRM, and docs tools.

  • Ops automation

    Notices, invoices, and compliance mail triggered from systems — not printers on desks.

  • Regulated senders

    Keep formal mail options (including Certified) without a parallel vendor stack.
