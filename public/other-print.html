FAQ

Does USMail.ai print business cards, brochures, flyers, or signs?

No. USMail.ai is print-to-mail: documents, postcards, and Certified Mail. Broadstroke, Inc. can quote business cards, brochures, flyers, signs, and other promo. Lead times are longer than mail. Not the app path. Email info@usmail.ai.

Can I order promo on the USMail.ai app?

No. The app is print-to-mail only. Promo print is a Broadstroke quote, not an upload-approve-produce mail job.
