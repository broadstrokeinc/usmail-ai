Mail date and what this path is not

Cutoffs are Central, Monday through Friday. Same-day when the job is eligible: approve by 1:00 PM CT. Default: 10:00 PM CT for the next business-day mail date. You can pick a later weekday up to 60 days. Certified is never same-day. Add it before you approve. It is not for postcards. Cutoff rules →

It is not Every Door Direct Mail. It is not a statement generator. Domestic USPS is the default. Do not assume Certified, tracing, CASS, or NCOA on international pieces.

Print and mail FAQ

What is USMail.ai print and mail?

Print and mail here means one production job. You upload documents and postcards. AI proposes setup. You approve. We print, insert, apply postage, and deliver the pieces to USPS. It is not a local print shop.

Is this mailing services in Wichita?

No. USMail.ai is a nationwide product. Wichita is the operator address, not a local print-and-mail shop. About →

Do you offer EDDM?

No. This path is documents and postcards from files you upload, with Certified when you select it. It is not Every Door Direct Mail.

Can I send international mail?

Domestic USPS is the default. Do not assume Certified Mail, IMB tracing, CASS, or NCOA on international pieces.

Are CASS, NCOA, and tracing included?

Yes. CASS and NCOA are included on every piece. Tracing is included on qualifying First-Class letters and postcards. Tracing is not tracking, not Certified proof, and not Marketing Mail. Address verification → · Tracing →

How do I pay for production mail?

Account plus prepaid meter. Approve on the app debits it. Production mail is not free. We do not publish unit prices.
