Print and mail FAQ

What is USMail.ai print and mail?

Print and mail here means one production job. You upload files and lists. You approve. We print, insert, apply postage, and hand the pieces to USPS. It is not a local print shop.

Is this mailing services in Wichita?

No. The product is nationwide. Operator address is Wichita. About →

Do you offer EDDM?

No. This path is documents and postcards from files you upload, with Certified when you select it. It is not Every Door Direct Mail.

Can I send international mail?

Domestic USPS is the default. International-capable where the stack supports it — ask ops. Do not assume Certified or IMB tracing on international pieces.
