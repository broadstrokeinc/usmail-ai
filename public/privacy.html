Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026 · Live platform site

Broadstroke, Inc. (“we,” “us”) operates USMail.ai at www.usmail.ai (820 W 2nd St N, Wichita, KS 67203). This policy describes how we handle information you provide when you contact us or create an account.

Data summary

Data Source Purpose Retention
Email, optional name / interest Early-access form Contact, account requests Until you request deletion or we close the list
Phone call content You call mail ops Sales / ops conversation Normal business records practice
Technical logs Hosting (Railway / CDN) Security, reliability Provider defaults / short operational windows

Information we collect

  • Get-started form: email address (required), name and interest category (optional), and technical metadata such as approximate time of submission and user-agent.
  • Contact: if you call or email us, we receive the information you choose to share (phone number, email content, etc.).
  • Server logs: standard request logs may include IP address and path for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To notify you when USMail.ai becomes available and to respond to demo / early-access requests.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Storage & retention

Form submissions are stored securely for account setup and sales follow-up. You may request deletion by emailing info@usmail.ai.

Third parties

The site may load fonts or icon scripts from third-party CDNs to render the page. Those providers process technical requests according to their own policies.
