Security overview

Last updated: August 19, 2026 · www.usmail.ai and app.usmail.ai

Broadstroke, Inc. operates USMail.ai. Production mail runs through app.usmail.ai. This marketing site is www.usmail.ai. No compliance seals we have not earned.

Live today

  • Live
    Account authentication and a prepaid meter. Jobs do not produce until you fund the meter and approve the job.
  • Live
    Human approval before production. You review on-screen proofs. We do not routinely screen your content.
  • Live
    Card numbers are handled by the payment gateway. USMail never stores card numbers.
  • Live
    Job files and recipient lists are used only to produce your mail. They remain your property. Data is purged after the window you select or our stated purge period.
  • Live
    HTTPS on www and the app. This site sends HSTS, CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, and frame protections. The app is not embedded here — it refuses framing.
  • Live
    Password-protected accounts. We will never ask for your password in an unsolicited call or email. Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.
  • Live
    Upload through the app, not email. Restrict portal access to people who should send mail. Remove access when someone leaves.
  • Live
    MCP is account-tied and billed on the same meter. No anonymous mailing. Standalone public API keys are not available yet.
  • Live
    Mail ops by phone and email — 888-667-5322 / 316-247-5300 / info@usmail.ai.

How we handle your data

We act as a conduit for print and mail. Lists and documents are processed to produce the job you approved — not for other marketing. We may disclose information to USPS or law enforcement when required or as described in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Production uses the Postalocity print-to-mail stack operated by Broadstroke. Deeper environment detail is available under NDA / security questionnaire for qualified buyers.

What we will not claim

  • Not claimed
    Live SOC 2 Type II (or other) certification for USMail.ai unless we publish a real report.
  • Not claimed
    HIPAA BAA availability until a signed program exists and is offered in writing.
  • Not claimed
    That we read every mail piece, or that approving a job means we endorse the content.
  • Not claimed
    That USMail.ai is the U.S. Postal Service, or that we control USPS delivery after handoff.
