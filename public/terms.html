Description of USMail

USMail.ai has been designed and developed to meet the postal mailing needs of individuals, businesses, local governments, and nonprofits through a Web interface. It allows small and large volume mailings alike to be uploaded and submitted to be printed and mailed by USMail. You upload documents. The system may propose setup. You approve. We print, insert, apply postage, and hand off to the United States Postal Service (“USPS”).

USMail is an all-inclusive service, meaning that physical materials (for example papers and envelopes), production procedures (for example folding, inserting, sorting), and USPS postage are included in the final on-screen price. To acquire an accurate job quote, the job must be entered and processed by USMail to full readiness for mailing. When the price is accepted, payment is made and the job is entered into production. If the price is not accepted, the job may be deleted without any costs incurred.

USMail generally delivers mail to the USPS the next business day after an order has been placed using the USMail service. Small-volume jobs are eligible to have the mailing date backdated to same-day service if entered and approved prior to 1:00 p.m. Central Time. “Small-volume” is an arbitrary number that may be adjusted from time to time by USMail but not to exceed one hundred unless expanded for special needs of individual clients. Same-day mailing is not guaranteed for all small-volume jobs and may be unavailable during high-demand mailing dates or immediately preceding USPS holiday closings. If same-day service is available, the current date in the calendar will be open for selection when approving your job.
